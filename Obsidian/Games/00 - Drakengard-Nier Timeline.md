# Drakengard/NieR Timeline Hub

A comprehensive guide to the interconnected Drakengard and NieR universe.

> [!info] Universe Overview
> The Drakengard and NieR series are connected through multiple timelines and endings. This hub organizes lore, characters, and events across all games in chronological and release order.

---

## Chronological Order (In-Universe Timeline)

1. **Drakengard 3** - The beginning of the timeline
2. **Drakengard 1** - First game released, continues the story
3. **Drakengard 2** - Alternative timeline (non-canon to NieR)
4. **NieR Replicant/Gestalt** - Born from Drakengard 1's Ending E
5. **NieR Automata** - Far future continuation
