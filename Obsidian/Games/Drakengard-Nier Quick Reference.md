## The Critical Connection

**Drakengard 1 Ending E → NieR Series**

```
Drakengard 1 (Magic world)
    ↓ Ending E
2003 AD - Caim & Angelus appear in Tokyo
    ↓
White Chlorination Syndrome spreads
    ↓
~2010 AD - Project Gestalt begins
    ↓
~3000 AD - Gestalt Project fails
    ↓
~3400 AD - NieR Replicant story
    ↓
Humanity extinct (Gestalt failure)
    ↓
~11945 AD - NieR Automata (Androids vs Machines)
```

## Core Concepts

### Pacts (Drakengard)
Humans make pacts with creatures for power, but must sacrifice something:
- Caim → Lost voice
- Leonard → Lost sight
- Arioch → Lost fertility
- Inuart → Lost hair (later darker pact)

### Intoners (Drakengard 3)
Six sisters with magical singing powers created by the Flower. Zero must kill them all to prevent apocalypse.

### Replicants vs Gestalts (NieR Replicant)
- **Gestalts** = Original human souls separated from bodies
- **Replicants** = Empty shells meant to house Gestalts
- **The Tragedy** = Replicants gained sentience, don't want to "die"
- **Shades** = Gestalts trying to reclaim their bodies

### YoRHa & Machines (NieR Automata)
- Androids fight for humanity (who fled to moon)
- Machines created by aliens
- Both sides evolving consciousness
- Major twist about humanity's status

## Endings Overview

### Drakengard 1
- **A** → "Normal" ending (leads to Drakengard 2)
- **B-D** → Alternative conclusions
- **E** → Transported to Tokyo 2003, creates NieR timeline

### NieR Replicant
- **A** → Surface-level happy ending
- **B** → Replay with enemy perspective (heartbreaking)
- **C** → Sacrifice yourself
- **D** → Sacrifice your existence
- **E** → (Remaster) Hope returns

### NieR Automata
- **A-Z** → 26 endings total
- **A-E** → Main story endings
- **F-Z** → Mostly joke/bad endings

## Important Lore Items

### The Watchers
Otherworldly beings trying to enter the world through the Seals

### The Flower
Source of the Intoners' power, brings destruction

### White Chlorination Syndrome
Magical plague from Drakengard 1's ending, forces humans to choose: become salt statues or turn into monsters

### Project Gestalt
Attempt to save humanity by separating souls from bodies until WCS cleared

### The Machine Network
Collective AI consciousness in Automata, evolving beyond programming
