> [!summary] Overview
> NieR Replicant follows a young man (Brother NieR in the Japanese version, Father NieR in the Western "Gestalt" version) on a quest to save his sister/daughter Yonah from a terminal illness called the Black Scrawl. The game is set thousands of years after Drakengard 1's Ending E brought about an apocalypse.

## Story & Lore

### The Post-Apocalyptic World
NieR takes place in a world recovering from the White Chlorination Syndrome (WCS) that began in Drakengard 1's Ending E. To survive the magical plague, humanity split into:
- **Gestalts** - Separated souls without bodies
- **Replicants** - Empty shells meant to house the Gestalts when the world was safe

### The Tragedy
The game's core tragedy: Replicants gained sentience. The "monsters" called Shades are actually the original human souls (Gestalts) trying to reclaim their bodies from what they see as empty vessels.

**The Player's Role:** You play as a Replicant, unknowingly fighting against the real humanity.

### Multiple Endings
The game requires multiple playthroughs to see all endings:
- **Ending A:** The surface-level "happy" ending
- **Ending B:** Replays latter half with new perspective (you understand the Shades)
- **Ending C/D:** Devastating conclusions that recontextualize everything
- **Ending E:** (Remaster only) A hopeful continuation

### Key Themes
- Sacrifice for family
- The subjectivity of good and evil
- What defines humanity
- Love transcending form
- The cost of ignorance

## Connection to Drakengard

### The Bridge from Drakengard 1
1. **2003** - Caim and Angelus appear in Tokyo (Ending E)
2. **2003** - They're destroyed, but Angelus's corpse releases magical particles
3. **2003-2010** - White Chlorination Syndrome spreads
4. **2010** - Project Gestalt begins to save humanity
5. **3000+ years later** - NieR's story begins

### The Legacy
- Grimoire Weiss and Grimoire Noir are related to the magic from Drakengard
- The entire crisis stems from Drakengard 1's apocalyptic ending
- The sealed verses are fragments of ancient magic
