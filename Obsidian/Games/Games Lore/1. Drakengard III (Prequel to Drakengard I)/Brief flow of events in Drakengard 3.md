# Drakengard 3 - Story Flow

**Game:** [[00 - Drakengard III Hub|Drakengard 3]]

> [!info] Spoiler Warning
> This document contains story summaries. Read based on your progress.

---

## Prologue: The Beginning

**Year:** ~990 AD (approximately)

Zero awakens with a mysterious Flower growing from her right eye. She has one goal: kill her five sisters, the Intoners (One, Two, Three, Four, and Five), who rule over different lands.

**The Intoners:**
Each sister possesses magical singing abilities and rules a territory with their Disciple (male companion/warrior):
- **One** & Disciple Brother
- **Two** & Disciple Cent
- **Three** & Disciple Octa
- **Four** & Disciple Dito
- **Five** & Disciple Partition

Zero is accompanied by **Mikhail**, a young dragon she found as a baby.

---

## Branch A: The Main Campaign

### Hunting Five
Zero's first target is her youngest sister, **Five**, who rules the Land of Seas.

**Events:**
- Zero and Mikhail track down Five
- Discovers Five is childish, hedonistic, and obsessed with pleasure
- After fierce battle, Five is killed along with her disciple Partition
- Zero absorbs Five's power

**Key Moment:** Zero shows no remorse, only determination to continue her mission.

### Pursuing Four
Next target: **Four**, the child-like sister who rules the Forest Land.

**Events:**
- Four appears innocent and naive, likes dolls and her disciple Dito
- Despite her childish demeanor, she's a formidable opponent
- Zero defeats and kills Four, absorbing her power
- Dito also falls in battle

**Observation:** Each sister's death is brutal - Zero shows no mercy.

### Confronting Three
Zero moves on to **Three**, the intellectual sister who rules the Desert Land.

**Events:**
- Three is scholarly and analytical, paired with disciple Octa
- She attempts to reason with Zero, seeking to understand why Zero wants them dead
- Zero refuses to explain, only fights
- Three and Octa are defeated and killed

**Pattern Emerges:** Zero is systematically eliminating her sisters, growing stronger with each death.

### Destroying Two
**Two** rules the Sands of Illusion, obsessed with beauty and perfection.

**Events:**
- Two is vain and narcissistic, constantly pursuing aesthetic perfection
- Her disciple Cent serves her obsessively
- Two fights to preserve her beauty and power
- Zero kills both Two and Cent

**Zero's Condition:** The Flower is growing, causing Zero increasing pain.

### Final Sister: One
The eldest and most powerful, **One** rules the Cathedral City with disciple Brother.

**Events:**
- One is calm, elegant, and mysterious
- She seems to know more about the Flower than she reveals
- Most powerful of all the Intoners
- Brother is the strongest disciple

**Branch A Ending:**
Zero defeats One, but something goes wrong. The ending is ambiguous and unsatisfying, hinting that the story isn't complete.

---

## Branch B: The Truth Deepens

Branch B reveals more about the Flower and introduces a mysterious figure who appears in Zero's dreams/visions.

**New Information:**
- The Flower is not just a mark - it's a parasitic entity
- The Intoners' power comes from the Flower
- Zero's mission is connected to preventing a catastrophe

**Mikhail's Growth:** The dragon grows stronger and more bonded to Zero.

---

## Branch C: Darker Revelations

Branch C shows the story from different perspectives and reveals Zero's backstory.

**The Flower's Origin:**
- Zero was once human, dying from violence
- The Flower attached to her as she died
- It saved her life but gave her magical power
- The Flower's goal: use the Intoners to destroy the world

**Creating Sisters:**
- Zero didn't birth her sisters naturally
- The Flower split its power, creating the five Intoners
- Zero realized the Flower's destructive nature and tried to kill herself
- When that failed, the "sisters" were born from the separated powers

**Zero's True Mission:**
She must kill her sisters and then herself to destroy the Flower completely and prevent apocalypse.

---

## Branch D: The Complete Picture

Branch D fills in remaining gaps.

**The Disciples:**
Each disciple is bound to their Intoner through song and magic. They're necessary for the Intoners to maintain control.

**The Flower's Plan:**
The Flower seeks to open a gateway for otherworldly entities (the Watchers) to enter the world.

**Zero's Sacrifice:**
Killing her sisters means taking their power (and Flower essence) into herself, making her the only target that needs to be destroyed.

---

## Branch E (Final Ending): The Ultimate Sacrifice

Branch E contains the true ending and the hardest challenge in the game.

**The Setup:**
- Zero has killed all her sisters
- She has absorbed all their power and all the Flower's essence
- Now only Zero herself must die to end the threat

**One's Survival:**
Zero discovers **One** survived their previous encounter. One understands what must be done.

**The Flower Takes Over:**
The Flower, sensing danger, tries to possess Zero completely. Zero fights for control.

**The Final Battle:**
One of gaming's most infamous final bosses - a rhythm game sequence where Brother must kill the Flower-possessed Zero by singing her song perfectly.

**The Outcome:**
- If successful: Brother kills Zero, destroying the Flower
- The world is saved from the apocalyptic threat
- However, the use of magic and the events set up the world for Drakengard 1
- The cycle of violence and magical corruption continues

**Connection to Drakengard 1:**
The events of Drakengard 3 explain why magic exists in Drakengard 1's world and hint at the involvement of the Watchers (who appear more directly in Drakengard 1).

---

## Key Themes

### Necessary Evil
Zero commits terrible acts (killing her sisters) to prevent greater catastrophe. The ends justify the means.

### Sacrifice
Zero must not only kill her family but ultimately give her own life to save the world.

### Corruption of Power
The Flower represents how power corrupts and destroys. Even the gift of survival came with apocalyptic cost.

### Gray Morality
- Zero is the protagonist but murders her sisters
- The sisters aren't pure evil - they're victims of the Flower
- No one is truly good or evil

## Timeline Context

**Position in Universe:**
Chronologically first - these events establish the magical world that Drakengard 1 inherits.

**Lead-in to Drakengard 1:**
The destruction of the Flower doesn't end magic entirely. The seeds of magical corruption remain, setting up the world of Seals and Watchers in Drakengard 1.
