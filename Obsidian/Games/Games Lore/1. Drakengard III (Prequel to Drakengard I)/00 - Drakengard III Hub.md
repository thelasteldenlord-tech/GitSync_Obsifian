## Story & Lore

### Main Story
Zero awakens with a flower growing from her right eye and a singular goal: kill her five sisters. Each sister is an Intoner with magical singing abilities and rules over different lands with their male disciples.

**The Intoners:**
1. **One** - The eldest and most powerful
2. **Two** - Obsessed with beauty and perfection
3. **Three** - The scholarly sister
4. **Four** - Child-like and innocent
5. **Five** - The lustful and hedonistic sister

### Endings
The game features multiple endings that reveal deeper truths about the Flower, the Intoners, and the fate of the world. The final ending connects to the events that precede Drakengard 1.

### Key Themes
- Sacrifice and duty
- The cost of preventing catastrophe
- Gray morality and necessary evil
- The corruption of power
