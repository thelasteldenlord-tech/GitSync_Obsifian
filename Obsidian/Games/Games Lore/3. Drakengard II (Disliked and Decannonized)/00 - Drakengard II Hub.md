## Story & Lore

### Main Story
Set 18 years after Drakengard 1's first ending, the world is under the control of the Knights of the Seal, a tyrannical organization that arose to maintain order. Nowe, a young man raised by a dragon, becomes caught in a conflict that will determine the fate of humanity.

### Timeline Branch
```
Drakengard 1 → Ending A → Drakengard 2 (Alternative timeline)
            ↓ Ending E
         NieR Timeline (Canon continuation)
```

### Why It's Considered Non-Canon
- Yoko Taro was not involved as director
- The tone is lighter and more conventional than other games in the series
- It doesn't connect to the NieR games
- Fan reception was mixed

### Key Differences
- More traditional JRPG storytelling
- Lighter tone compared to Drakengard 1
- Focus on redemption rather than moral ambiguity
- Simplified pact system
