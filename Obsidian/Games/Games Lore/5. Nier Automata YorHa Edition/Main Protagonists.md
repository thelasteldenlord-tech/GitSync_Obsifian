# NieR Automata - Main Protagonists

**Game:** [[00 - Nier Automata Hub|NieR Automata]]

---

## 2B (YoRHa No. 2 Type B)

**Full Designation:** YoRHa No. 2 Type B
**Model Type:** Battle Android
**Height:** 168cm (5'6")
**Weight:** 148.8kg (328 lbs)

### Overview
2B is a combat model android sent from the Bunker to fight machines on Earth. She is calm, composed, and professional, maintaining emotional distance from others. She wields two swords and fights with elegant, deadly precision.

**Appearance:**
- Black dress (YoRHa uniform)
- White/silver hair in bob cut
- Blindfold (standard YoRHa equipment, actually a tactical visor)
- Black gloves and boots

### Personality
Stoic and mission-focused, 2B keeps her emotions tightly controlled. She appears cold but demonstrates care for her companion 9S, though she tries to maintain professional boundaries. She's bound by duty and follows orders without question.

### Combat Style
- **Primary:** Twin sword wielding
- **Pod Support:** Pod 042 provides ranged attacks
- **Special:** Berserk mode available

### Story Role
2B serves as the player's introduction to the world of NieR Automata. Her perspective in Route A shows the surface conflict between androids and machines.

> [!warning] Major Spoiler
> 2B's designation actually stands for "Type E" (Executioner). Her true mission is to kill 9S whenever he learns too much about Project YoRHa's secrets.

## 9S (YoRHa No. 9 Type S)

**Full Designation:** YoRHa No. 9 Type S
**Model Type:** Scanner Android
**Height:** 160cm (5'3")
**Weight:** 129.9kg (286 lbs)

### Overview
9S is a reconnaissance android designed for information gathering and hacking. Unlike combat models, he's more emotional and curious about the world. He forms a close bond with 2B despite her attempts to maintain distance.

**Appearance:**
- White/cream YoRHa uniform
- White hair, shorter than 2B
- Blindfold (tactical visor)
- More lightweight build than combat models

### Personality
Kind, curious, and emotionally expressive. 9S questions things and seeks to understand rather than simply destroy. He's optimistic and friendly, often trying to engage 2B in conversation. However, he has a darker side that emerges when pushed to extremes.

### Combat Style
- **Primary:** Single sword combat (less proficient than 2B)
- **Specialty:** Hacking - can hack machines and turn them into allies or detonate them
- **Pod Support:** Pod 153

### Story Role
9S provides a different perspective in Route B through hacking insights. Route C reveals his psychological breakdown and descent into darkness when he loses what he cares about most.

**Key Trait:** His scanner designation means he's prone to discovering classified information, which makes him a target for "disposal" by Type E units.

## A2 (YoRHa No. 2 Type A)

**Full Designation:** YoRHa No. 2 Type A
**Model Type:** Attacker Prototype Android
**Height:** 168cm (5'6")
**Weight:** 151.8kg (334 lbs)

### Overview
A2 is a prototype combat android and deserter from YoRHa. She's older than the current YoRHa models and has long since abandoned the organization. Gruff and solitary, she wanders Earth fighting machines on her own terms.

**Appearance:**
- Tattered YoRHa outfit (damaged and worn)
- Long white hair
- No blindfold (discarded along with her YoRHa identity)
- Battle-scarred and weathered

### Personality
Cynical, blunt, and anti-social. A2 has been through trauma and betrayal, leaving her bitter toward YoRHa. Despite her rough exterior, she shows compassion in unexpected moments, particularly toward Pascal and his village.

### Combat Style
- **Primary:** Heavy sword combat with aggressive style
- **Special:** Berserk mode - trades defense for massive attack power
- **Unique:** No pod initially, more direct combat focus

### Story Role
A2 appears as an antagonist initially but becomes playable in Route C. Her story reveals the dark truth about the Pearl Harbor Descent mission and Project YoRHa's real purpose.

**Background:**
A2 was part of the first YoRHa deployment, sent on a suicide mission to test the viability of YoRHa units. Most of her squad was killed, and she learned YoRHa was designed to fail. She deserted and has been on the run ever since.

## Support Characters

### Pod 042
2B's support unit. Provides ranged weapons, information analysis, and strategic advice. Despite being a machine, Pod 042 develops beyond its programming.

### Pod 153
9S's support unit. Similar to Pod 042 but with slightly different personality parameters. Shows concern for 9S's well-being.

### Commander White
Leader of the YoRHa forces from the Bunker. Calm and authoritative, she carries the burden of knowing Project YoRHa's true purpose.

### Operator 6O
9S's enthusiastic operator who frequently contacts him with mission updates and personal chatter. She's friendly and curious about Earth.

### Operator 21O
2B's professional operator. More formal than 6O, she maintains strict protocol.

---

## Character Dynamics

### 2B ↔ 9S
Complex relationship of care, duty, and tragedy. 2B is assigned to kill 9S repeatedly whenever he learns too much. Despite this, genuine feelings develop between them.

### 9S ↔ A2
Initially enemies (9S wants revenge for thinking A2 killed 2B), their paths mirror each other - both lose everything and must choose how to respond.

### A2 ↔ 2B
A2 recognizes 2B as similar to her former squad members. Their fighting styles are nearly identical, suggesting A2's Type A was the predecessor to Type B/E models.

### All Three ↔ Machines
Begin seeing machines as enemies, gradually discover machines and androids are more similar than different.
