# NieR Automata Hub

**Release Year:** 2017 (Original), 2018 (YoRHa Edition with DLC)
**Platform:** PS4, Xbox One, PC, Switch
**Developer:** PlatinumGames
**Director:** Yoko Taro

> [!summary] Overview
> NieR Automata is set thousands of years after NieR Replicant in a world where humanity has fled to the moon and sent androids to fight against alien-created machines that have conquered Earth. The game explores consciousness, existence, and what it means to be human through the eyes of androids 2B, 9S, and A2.

## Story & Lore

### The World of 11945 AD
- Humanity fled to the moon after NieR Replicant's events
- Aliens invaded Earth and created machine lifeforms
- Androids wage an eternal proxy war on behalf of humanity
- Earth is a beautiful ruin reclaimed by nature

### Three Routes = One Complete Story
- **Route A (2B's Perspective):** The surface mission and combat
- **Route B (9S's Perspective):** Same events, new insights via hacking
- **Route C/D/E:** The truth revealed, multiple endings

### The Central Mystery
Without spoilers for new players, the game explores:
- The truth about humanity's status
- The YoRHa project's real purpose
- What the machine network truly wants
- How consciousness emerges in artificial beings

### Major Themes
- **Glory to mankind** - What does this really mean?
- Consciousness and sentience
- The cycle of hatred and violence
- Finding meaning in a meaningless world
- Love and sacrifice
- What separates machines from humans

## Gameplay

### Combat System
Developed by PlatinumGames (Bayonetta, Metal Gear Rising):
- Fast, fluid hack-and-slash combat
- Multiple weapon types and combos
- Pod programs (ranged attacks and abilities)
- Hacking mechanic (as 9S) - bullet-hell style mini-games
- Chip system for customization

### RPG Elements
- Level progression
- Plug-in chips (modify stats and abilities)
- Weapon upgrades
- Multiple endings (26 endings total, including joke endings)

## Connection to Previous Games

### Timeline Position
~10,000+ years after NieR Replicant

### Legacy of NieR Replicant
- [[../4. Nier Replicant - American Version/00 - Nier Replicant Hub|NieR Replicant]] ended humanity's chance to recombine
- Gestalt project failed
- New form of "humanity" emerged
- Replicants may have evolved into androids (fan theory)

- Part of the same universe chain starting from Drakengard 1 Ending E
- References to old world magic and technology
- The cycle of violence continues

- **Emil** - A character from NieR Replicant makes a significant appearance
- **Lunar Tears** - Flowers from NieR Replicant
