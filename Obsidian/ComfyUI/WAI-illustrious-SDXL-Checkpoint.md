## Discussion

Long-time fan of this model, and the v16 update is really nice. The new name fits and the strengths are still here: anime-accurate, strong details, and works well for both clean and mature artwork. One of the best SDXL anime models available imho.

Even with a good model, civitai seems to require a yellow buzz simply by entering an arounsed expression in the prompt. Sometimes, even if you think the prompt is fine, pressing the generate button suddenly triggers a yellow buzz. Many users have posted similar complaints, and the issue has become a lively discussion topic on Reddit's r/civitai and in Civitai articles and posts.

Community Response

Similar comments are commonplace, with many criticizing the prompt's NSFW designation and Buzz switching. For example, there are frequent reports of forced Yellow Buzz for words like "arounsed expression," and many criticize the ambiguity of the admin's automated detection.

We hope these comments will help the admin make improvements.

Have been using v14 for a long time, skipped v15 because personally, feels that the color is too strong and oversaturated.

Tried using the v16 and so far am happy with the results. Color is back to to v14, but with better prompt accuracy. Though still having problem with double and rarely triple eyelids, also Untucked shirt! (even with shirt tucked in as negative). Fingers are still 50/50, AI-kun really hate fingers. Also, see-through different part of clothes, like when I put off-shoulder dress + wedding dress and layered dress + turtleneck + see-through dress layer with bridal gauntlets, sometimes it make gloves see-through instead of turtleneck part:s.

Sorry if it sound like complaint. But all in all, I believe its better than v14, and am migrating to this now.

After several days of testing, I found that prompts designed to induce anime coloring (not only *anime coloring* itself, but the anime coloring art style) didn't work well. In V15, adding *anime coloring* in the negative prompt helped suppress the issue to some degree.
