## Requesting Policy Changes

If you believe a policy needs updating:
1. Document the issue or concern
2. Propose specific changes
3. Submit to management
4. Policies will be reviewed within 30 days

---

## Emergency Policy Violations

**If you witness or suspect a policy violation**:
1. Report immediately to management
2. Document what you observed
3. Do not confront the person directly
4. Protect any affected data/systems

See [[Policies/Incident Response]] for detailed procedures.
