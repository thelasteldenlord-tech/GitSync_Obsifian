# Data Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: 2025-10-28
**Last Updated**: 2025-10-28
**Next Review**: 2026-01-28

---

## Purpose

This policy establishes how our company collects, uses, stores, and protects all data, with particular emphasis on client information and personally identifiable information (PII).

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and agents
- All data collected, regardless of source or sensitivity
- All storage locations (physical, digital, cloud)
- All business operations and client interactions

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Data Minimization
**Collect only what is necessary.**

- Gather only the minimum data needed for business purposes
- Justify each data point collected
- Regularly purge unnecessary information
- Question whether data collection is truly required

### 2. Purpose Limitation
**Use data only for stated purposes.**

- Collect data for specific, legitimate business needs
- Do not repurpose data without client consent
- Document the purpose of each data collection
- Respect the original intent

### 3. Data Security
**Protect all data as if it were our own.**

- Implement technical and administrative safeguards
- Follow [[Understanding Security Systems]] framework
- Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit
- Limit access based on need-to-know principle

### 4. Transparency
**Be honest about data practices.**

- Inform clients what data we collect
- Explain how data will be used
- Disclose who has access
- Provide data upon client request

### 5. Accountability
**Take responsibility for data protection.**

- Each employee is responsible for protecting data
- Report breaches immediately
- Document all data handling procedures
- Accept consequences for violations

## Data Classification

### Highly Sensitive (Restricted Access)
**Storage**: `clients/prospects/highly_sensitive/`
**Access**: Confidential group only

Includes:
- Social Security Numbers
- Credit card information
- Financial records (income, credit reports)
- Medical information
- Passwords and credentials
- Legal documents (contracts, NDAs)

**Handling Requirements**:
- Encrypted storage mandatory
- Access logged and audited
- Never transmitted via unencrypted channels
- Immediate deletion when no longer needed

### Confidential (Limited Access)
**Storage**: `clients/` directories
**Access**: Owners and Managers

Includes:
- Client contact information
- Property details
- Business relationships
- Pricing and proposals
- Meeting notes
- Communication history

**Handling Requirements**:
- Access controls enforced
- Not shared outside company
- Secured during transmission
- Regular access reviews

### Internal Use (Staff Access)
**Storage**: `operations/` directories
**Access**: Appropriate staff groups

Includes:
- Schedules and assignments
- Procedures and policies
- Internal communications
- Non-sensitive operational data

**Handling Requirements**:
- Group-based access controls
- Professional use only
- Not shared externally

### Public Information
Information already in public domain, but still treated respectfully.

**Handling Requirements**:
- Still subject to confidentiality
- Not republished or shared casually
- Treated as client information

## Data Collection

### Permitted Sources
- Direct client communication
- Public records and databases
- Publicly available business information
- Client-provided documents
- Authorized third-party sources

### Prohibited Methods
- Deception or misrepresentation
- Unauthorized system access
- Social engineering of client staff
- Illegal surveillance or recording
- Bribery or coercion

### Documentation Required
For each data collection:
- Source of information
- Date collected
- Purpose and justification
- Authorized collector
- Client awareness (if applicable)

## Data Storage

### Physical Storage
- Locked file cabinets for paper documents
- Restricted access to storage areas
- Clean desk policy (no sensitive docs left out)
- Secure disposal (shredding)

### Digital Storage
- Follow [[Understanding Security Systems]] framework
- Group-based access controls
- Regular backups (encrypted)
- Cloud storage: only approved, encrypted services
- No storage on personal devices

### Retention Periods
| Data Type | Retention Period | Disposal Method |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| Client contracts | 7 years after termination | Secure destruction |
| Financial records | 7 years | Secure destruction |
| Meeting notes | Duration of relationship + 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Proposals (accepted) | Life of contract + 7 years | Secure destruction |
| Proposals (rejected) | 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Prospect data (no sale) | 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Highly sensitive data | Only as long as necessary | Immediate secure destruction |

## Data Access

### Access Principles
- **Need-to-know basis only**
- Minimum privilege required for job function
- Regular access reviews (quarterly)
- Immediate revocation upon role change or departure

### Accessing Client Data
Before accessing client data, verify:
1. You have a legitimate business need
2. You have appropriate authorization
3. Your access is logged
4. You understand handling requirements

**All access to highly sensitive data is logged and audited.**

### Sharing Data Internally
- Only with authorized personnel
- Only for legitimate business purposes
- Use secure methods (no email for highly sensitive)
- Document the sharing and reason

### Sharing Data Externally
**Generally prohibited** unless:
- Client provides written consent
- Required by law (with legal review)
- Shared with authorized service provider (with NDA)
- Public information being confirmed

**Never share**:
- Highly sensitive information externally
- Client data with competitors
- Information to family or friends
- Data on social media

## Data Subject Rights

Clients have the right to:
- **Access**: Request copies of their data
- **Correction**: Request corrections to inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of their data (with exceptions)
- **Portability**: Receive data in portable format
- **Objection**: Object to certain data processing

### Handling Client Requests
1. Verify identity of requester
2. Log the request
3. Respond within 30 days
4. Provide requested data or explain denial
5. Document the interaction

## Data Breach Response

### What Constitutes a Breach
- Unauthorized access to data
- Data theft or loss
- Accidental disclosure
- System compromise
- Physical document loss

### Immediate Actions
1. **Contain**: Stop the breach if ongoing
2. **Report**: Notify management immediately
3. **Document**: Record all details
4. **Preserve**: Don't delete logs or evidence
5. **Assess**: Determine scope and severity

### Management Response
1. Activate [[Policies/Incident Response]] procedures
2. Investigate the breach
3. Notify affected clients (if required)
4. Report to authorities (if required)
5. Implement corrective measures
6. Document lessons learned

**Do not attempt to cover up a breach. Immediate reporting is mandatory.**

- Sign acknowledgment form

### Owners Must Additionally
- Ensure policy compliance company-wide
- Review and update policies
- Approve policy exceptions (rare)

---

## Violations and Consequences

### Types of Violations
- **Minor**: Accidental, no harm, first offense
  - Example: Leaving document on desk overnight
  - Consequence: Written warning, retraining

- **Moderate**: Negligent, potential harm, or repeated minor
  - Example: Sharing client data with unauthorized staff
  - Consequence: Suspension, serious review

- **Severe**: Intentional, actual harm, or reckless
  - Example: Selling client data, intentional breach
  - Consequence: Immediate termination, possible legal action

### Reporting Violations
- Report to management immediately
- No retaliation for good-faith reporting
- Anonymous reporting available
- Protection for whistleblowers
