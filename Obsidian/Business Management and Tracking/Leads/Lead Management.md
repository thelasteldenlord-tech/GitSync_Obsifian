# Lead Management

> Central hub for tracking all business leads and prospects

## Lead Pipeline Overview

### Status Definitions
- **Prospect**: Initial research phase, not yet contacted
- **Contacted**: Initial contact made, awaiting response
- **Scheduled**: Meeting or call scheduled
- **Active**: Currently in negotiation or proposal phase
- **Contracted**: Deal closed, client onboarded
- **Rejected**: Not interested or not qualified
- **Follow-up**: Requires future contact

## Lead Qualification Criteria

### Key Factors
1. **Proximity** - Within service area
2. **Property size** - Adequate revenue potential
3. **Employee count** - Indicates facility size
4. **BBB rating** - Indicates professionalism
5. **Budget indicators** - Revenue/size estimates

### Disqualifiers
- Too far from service area
- Facility too small
- Already has established service
- Poor communication/unprofessional
