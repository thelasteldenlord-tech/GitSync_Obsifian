## How to Use Templates

### Creating New Documents
1. Open the template you need
2. Copy the entire content
3. Create a new note in the appropriate folder
4. Paste the template content
5. Replace all {{PLACEHOLDER}} text with actual information
6. Remove any sections that don't apply

### Template Placeholders
All templates use `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` format:
- `{{COMPANY_NAME}}` - Client company name
- `{{DATE}}` - Current date
- `{{YOUR_NAME}}` - Your name
- etc.

### Customizing Templates
Feel free to modify templates to fit your needs. To update a template:
1. Edit the template in the Templates folder
2. Changes will apply to future uses
3. Existing documents won't be affected
