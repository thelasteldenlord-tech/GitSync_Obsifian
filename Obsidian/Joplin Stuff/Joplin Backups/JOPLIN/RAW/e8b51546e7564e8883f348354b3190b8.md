🌑 Proto-Script: Seed Table (Draft 1)

Below is a first sketch of a symbolic seed table—30+ core primitives with their Unicode symbols and seed meanings. This is our “periodic table of meaning,” from which we can paint ideas like a brush on a canvas.

Existence & Being

⊙ (circled dot) → self, center, origin; identity, soul, source0

◯ (empty circle) → whole, cycle, sun; emptiness, eternity, cosmic

◍ (circle quartered) → phase, becoming; transition, growth, change

◐ (half circle) → polarity, dual state; waxing, waning, incomplete

∞ (infinity) → unbounded, eternal; paradox, divine, recursion


Matter & Elements

▵ (triangle up) → fire, rising, action; will, conflict, energy

▽ (triangle down) → water, falling, yielding; flow, receptivity

▱ (parallelogram) → earth, place, boundary; stability, law, material

✶ (star) → air, spirit, multiplicity; inspiration, cosmos

⌇ (triple bar) → waves, water, uncertainty; subconscious, dream, chaos

⟁ (triangle with dot) → spark, origin of fire; ignition, creation


Relation & Process

⇄ (arrows left-right) → exchange, flow; reciprocity, dialogue, commerce

⋔ (pitchfork) → division, opposition; tension, choice, crossroads

∴ (therefore) → cause, order, law; consequence, structure, destiny

☍ (opposition/aspect) → the other, duality; foreign, mirror, counterpart

↻ (circular arrow) → return, cycle, renewal; time, repetition

⚯ (interlocked circle & square) → union, integration; synthesis, harmony


Life & Agency

◉ (bullseye) → person, being; agent, individual, perspective

✿ (flower) → life, growth, fertility; beauty, renewal, organic

☠ (skull) → death, end; danger, cessation, transformation

⚑ (flag) → intent, claim; declaration, will, nation

⚕ (medical cross) → healing, restoration; balance, remedy

⚗ (alembic) → transformation, distillation; refinement, science, magic


Cosmos & Unknown

☀ (sun symbol) → illumination, clarity; consciousness, enlightenment

☾ (moon) → hidden, subconscious; dream, tides, cycles

✧ (sparkle) → subtle, ethereal; hint, possibility, mystery

⚝ (outlined star) → destiny, celestial map; guiding principle

⌆ (anchor) → grounding, stability; return, memory


Tools & Constructs

⚒ (hammer & pick) → work, craft, building; shaping, force

⚙ (gear) → mechanism, structure; process, system

⚐ (white flag) → pause, surrender; ceasefire, neutrality

⛬ (fortress) → protection, boundary; shelter, defense

⚖ (scales) → balance, justice; weighing, evaluation

⌛ (hourglass) → time, finitude; urgency, limit



---

Example Expressions

◉ ▵ ⚑ = person + fire + intent → warrior, revolutionary, passionate actor.

◯ ∴ ◉ = cosmos orders person → destiny, fate, astrology.

✿ ⇄ ⌇ = life exchanges with uncertainty → evolution, adaptation.

☠ ◯ ✶ = death within the cosmic starfield → transcendence, afterlife.

⚗ ⚒ ◍ = transformation + craft + becoming → alchemy of growth.



---

This is only a first brushstroke. Like paint, these marks can be combined, rotated, layered, and altered to compose deeper ideas. The point isn’t to fix rigid definitions but to give humans a visual grammar to sculpt meaning.
