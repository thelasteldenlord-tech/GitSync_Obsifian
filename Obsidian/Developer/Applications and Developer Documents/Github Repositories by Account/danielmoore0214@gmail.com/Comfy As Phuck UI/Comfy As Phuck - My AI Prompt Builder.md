
Technology: Mostly typescript and react, this app will load the custom word lists and other presets from the designated folder and make available as an autocompleting aide to build cohesive image generation prompts. It has a very sleek UI for a better UX than I normally offer, but I did have a ton of agentic help from Gemini 3 in Antigravity.

Why?: Because it can be damn hard on a total noob to dive into everything one must absorb to generate local uncensored images, text or video even, completely locally, with no internet access needed after setup and download of proper checkpoints and desired models and addons. Can't beat it. Better quality usually and a far more granular control over every step of the process.
