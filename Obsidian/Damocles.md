Damocles was a courtier in the ancient Greek court of Dionysius I of Syracuse, famous for the moral parable known as the 
**"Sword of Damocles".**

# The Flatterer: 
Damocles constantly praised King Dionysius, talking about how rich, powerful, and truly happy the ruler was.

# The Challenge: 
Tired of the flattery, Dionysius offered to switch places with Damocles so he could experience that wonderful life firsthand.

# The Trap:
Damocles sat at a grand banquet surrounded by fine food and luxury. He then looked up and saw a sharp sword hanging directly above his head, held up by only a single horsehair.

# The Lesson:
Dionysius wanted to show that anyone in deep power lives with constant fear, danger, and anxiety. Damocles begged to leave the feast and no longer envied the king.

# Meaning
The phrase "Sword of Damocles" is now a common idiom.
It represents an imminent, ever-present danger hanging over someone who has great power, wealth, or success.
