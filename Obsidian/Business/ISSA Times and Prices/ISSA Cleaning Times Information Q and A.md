

ISSA Cleaning Times provide industry-standard benchmarks for labor productivity, typically measuring tasks in minutes per square foot, linear foot, or per item (e.g., toilets, trash cans). These times, often used for bidding and staffing, are calculated based on trained workers with proper equipment, offering a baseline for tasks like vacuuming 10,000 sq ft/hour or cleaning restrooms. [[1](https://www.issa.com/articles/how-to-calculate-cleaning-times/#:~:text=You%20can%20calculate%20cleaning%20time%20by:%20*,you%20can%20contact%20cleaningtimes@issa.com%20for%20more%20information.), [2](https://www.issa.com/articles/workloading-finding-the-right-balance/), [3](https://www.scribd.com/document/64940650/Issa-Study)]

  

Key elements of ISSA cleaning times include:

- 540+ Specific Tasks: The 8th edition covers a vast array of tasks, including updated, specialized, and automated cleaning procedures.
- Methodology: Times are determined by averaging the performance of multiple cleaners performing the same task to find an realistic average.
- Factors Affecting Rates: Productivity is heavily influenced by facility type (e.g., healthcare vs. office), floor type, and occupant density.
- Calculation Formula: Total cleaning time is calculated as:
    
    $\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total Area (sq ft)}}{\text{Production Rate (sq ft/hour)}} \times 60 \text{ minutes} [4]$
    
      
    
- Resource Access: The data is available to ISSA members for $50 and non-members for $250 through their online learning system. _Example metrics:_

- Trash removal (15,000 sq ft): ~30 minutes.
- Vacuuming (Carpet): ~10,000 sq ft per hour.
- Riding Auto-scrubber: ~275 ft/minute.
